Possible design error in bin/xworkflows.mjs doctor
Root cause: the doctor keeps its own copy of the hook's rules (fresh by `main:` sha and `HEAD`, the cap and its default), and reads the config another way (`loadConfig` and `DEFAULTS` on error, where the hook uses `loadLenient`); the two already disagree on a config with one bad section, and the doctor's `HEAD` test has no test of its own (confirmation of 48872ff, findings 2 and 3; risk review of b7c534c, findings 1 and 6).
Alternative design: the doctor fires the `handoff-load` hook, adds none of its bytes to the session-start measure, and warns when its output says `was not loaded`; its copy at `bin/xworkflows.mjs:1011-1013` goes. Cost: one more node process per doctor run, about 3 lines fewer.

Possible design error in bin/xworkflows.mjs doctor
Root cause (confirmation of af38bcb, pending): the doctor reads the hook's state as a substring of its output, which also carries the hand-off text; a fresh hand-off under the cap that quotes "was not loaded" gets a false "shorten it" warning.
Alternative design: `hooks/core/handoff-load.mjs` exports its "not loaded" sentence and the doctor matches it where the context starts (a loaded hand-off always starts with `main: `). Cost: about 3 lines and one test.
